Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering with us

You can upload your prescription by navigating to the "Upload Prescription" section on our homepage. Simply take a clear photo or scan of your prescription, fill in your contact details, and submit. Our licensed pharmacists will review it within 2 hours and prepare your order.

We offer multiple delivery options: Same-day delivery for orders placed before 2 PM (available in select cities), next-day delivery for standard orders, and express 2-hour delivery for urgent needs. All orders come with real-time tracking.

Absolutely. We source all products directly from authorized manufacturers and distributors. Every item undergoes rigorous quality checks before dispatch. We maintain proper cold chain for temperature-sensitive products and verify batch numbers for authenticity.

We offer a 30-day return policy for unopened and unused products. For damaged or incorrect items, we provide free returns and full refunds. Prescription medicines cannot be returned once dispensed for safety reasons, but we will replace any defective items.

Yes, we accept most major health insurance plans. You can upload your insurance card during checkout, and we will process claims directly. We also offer HSA/FSA eligible products and provide itemized receipts for reimbursement.
